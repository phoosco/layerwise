How catalogue status works

CURATED Checked against manufacturer specifications or another identified reliable source.

UNVERIFIED Submitted by a user; visible to everyone as a contribution, but not treated as confirmed product data.

LOCAL WARDROBE Your personal gear and outfit selections are saved privately in your browser.

What happens to it?

  1. Your entry is saved to the shared Supabase database.
  2. It appears immediately in the catalogue as unverified for all visitors.
  3. It can be checked against the linked source.
  4. Once verified in Supabase, it upgrades to a curated record.
